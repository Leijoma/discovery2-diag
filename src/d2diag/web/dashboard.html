Help improve the tool?

You can share anonymous readings with the community so we can verify experimental mappings together and make the app better. We store a random anonymous ID and the protocol readings you choose to share — never your VIN, registration, location or any personal data, and never your IP address. Sharing is off unless you turn it on, and you can change this any time.
